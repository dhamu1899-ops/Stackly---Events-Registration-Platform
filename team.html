VISIONARY LEADERSHIP

Aravind Varma

Founder & Chief Creative Officer

With over 15 years orchestrating palace weddings and high-profile galas across India and Europe, Aravind founded Stackly in 2015 with a singular vision: to merge the timeless grandeur of heritage celebrations with seamless digital ticketing and guest hospitality.

Under his creative direction, Stackly has curated over 1,800 landmark weddings, milestone birthday galas, and cultural dance celebrations for dignitaries, celebrities, and discerning families worldwide.
