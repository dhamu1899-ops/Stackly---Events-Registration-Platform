COMMONLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning weddings, birthdays, dance nights, and baby naming ceremonies.

How early in advance should we reserve our wedding or celebration date? +
For peak wedding and anniversary seasons, we advise booking 3 to 6 months in advance to secure preferred palace venues and celebrity DJs. For birthdays and baby naming ceremonies, 3 to 4 weeks advance notice is generally sufficient.
Can we customize the decor themes for a baby naming or cradle ceremony? +
Absolutely! Our design team provides custom thematic cradle styling—ranging from traditional lotus and marigold temple arches to modern fairytale swings with customized baby monogram backdrops.
Do you provide sound permits and DJ equipment for late-night Sangeet dancing? +
Yes, our production team handles all local venue permissions, high-wattage acoustic licensing, acoustic baffling, and professional concert-grade lighting arrays.
